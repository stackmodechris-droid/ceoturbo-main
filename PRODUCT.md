# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

US business owners, founders, and CEOs who need to be easier to discover, easier to trust, and more memorable across search, social media, and in-person customer interactions. They are typically comparing providers quickly, often on a phone, and need a clear path from proof to a booked strategy call.

## Product Purpose

CEOTurbo sells a coordinated visibility system: custom-coded SEO, AEO, and GEO websites; Meta advertising for Facebook and Instagram; and branded physical products that connect real-world attention back to a website or honest Google review flow. Success means a visitor understands the offer within seconds, can verify the work through real supplied evidence, sees an exact price and scope, receives a sensible recommendation, and can call or book without friction.

## Brand Personality

Commanding, exact, kinetic. CEOTurbo should feel like a focused visibility command center operated by a hands-on founder: confident enough to publish real work and pricing, precise enough to state limitations, and energetic without becoming noisy.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated agency landing pages made from endless identical icon cards.
- Wix, Lovable, or page-builder template aesthetics that imply lock-in or interchangeable work.
- Hype-first marketing that promises rankings, leads, five-star reviews, or AI citations without evidence.
- Decorative glassmorphism, gradient text, purple-on-black neon, oversized pill cards, emoji icons, or motion that delays access to content.
- Galleries that crop, zoom, or hide supplied proof.

## Design Principles

- Show the proof before asking for trust.
- Make Search, Social, and Street feel like one connected visibility system.
- Publish the real scope, price, exclusions, and next step.
- Let each section answer one buyer question in plain language.
- Make ownership tangible: client-controlled code, GitHub, Vercel, and accounts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All navigation, galleries, dialogs, forms, and product demonstrations must work by keyboard and screen reader. Body copy must meet at least 4.5:1 contrast, focus must remain obvious, targets must remain usable on mobile, and every nonessential animation must provide a reduced-motion equivalent. No information may depend on color or motion alone.
